1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose
CampusNest is a web-based platform designed to help students in Cameroon find suitable accommodation near
universities and higher institutions. This document defines the functional and non-functional requirements for its
development.
1.2 Scope
The platform connects students with landlords, accommodation providers, approved repairers, cleaners, and
employers offering student-friendly jobs.
  Student accommodation search and booking inquiries
  Location-based accommodation discovery
  Accommodation listings and landlord management
  Student repair requests with landlord approval
  Approved repairer management
  Cleaning service requests
  Student job listings and applications
  User accounts, dashboards, and administration
 
1.3 Intended Users
  Students
  Landlords and property managers
  Accommodation providers
  Approved repairers
  Cleaners and cleaning companies
  Employers
  Platform administrators
 
1.4 Definitions
  FCFA: Central African CFA franc, the currency used for prices in Cameroon.
  Accommodation listing: A property or room advertised for student accommodation.
  Landlord approval: Permission granted by the property owner or authorized manager before a repair is assigned.
  Approved repairer: A repair service provider verified and authorized by the platform.
  Administrator: A user responsible for managing and monitoring the platform.
 
2. Overall Description
2.1 Product Perspective
CampusNest shall be a responsive web application accessible through smartphones, tablets, and desktop
computers. It shall use a centralized database to store user accounts, accommodation listings, service requests,
job posts, and administrative records.
2.2 Product Functions
  Search accommodation by institution, city, neighborhood, price, and type.

3. Functional Requirements
3.1 User Registration and Authentication
  FR-01: The system shall allow students to create accounts.
  FR-02: The system shall allow landlords, repairers, cleaners, and employers to register for appropriate account
 types.
 FR-03: The system shall allow users to log in and log out securely.
  FR-04: The system shall allow users to reset forgotten passwords.
  FR-05: The system shall restrict account functions according to user roles.
  FR-06: Administrators shall be able to suspend or deactivate accounts when necessary.
 
3.2 Accommodation Search
  FR-07: The system shall provide a search bar for accommodation discovery.
  FR-08: Users shall be able to search using institution names, cities, neighborhoods, or accommodation
 keywords.
 FR-09: A search such as “Hostels near RHIBMS” shall display accommodation listings near the specified
 institution.
 FR-10: Users shall be able to filter listings by budget, distance, accommodation type, availability, and furnished
 status.
 FR-11: The system shall display an appropriate message when no matching accommodation is found.
  FR-12: Search results shall be sortable by price, distance, and date added.

3.3 Accommodation Listings
  FR-13: Landlords shall be able to create accommodation listings.
  FR-14: A listing shall include property name, location, price, accommodation type, description, photographs, and
 availability.
 FR-15: Listings shall display prices in FCFA.
  FR-16: Landlords shall be able to edit, update, or remove their listings.
  FR-17: Administrators shall be able to review, approve, reject, or remove listings.
  FR-18: The system shall display the listing’s verification status.
  FR-19: Students shall be able to contact landlords or submit accommodation inquiries.
 
3.4 Interactive Map
  FR-20: The platform shall display accommodation locations on an interactive map of Cameroon.
  FR-21: Map markers shall represent accommodation listings.
  FR-22: Selecting a marker shall display a summary of the accommodation.
  FR-23: Users shall be able to zoom, pan, and explore locations.
  FR-24: The system shall support location searches by city, institution, and neighborhood.
  FR-25: Map locations shall be displayed only when valid geographic coordinates are available.
 
3.5 Repair and Maintenance Requests
  FR-26: Students shall be able to submit repair requests for their accommodation.
  FR-27: A request shall include the property, problem description, category, and optional photographs.
  FR-28: The system shall notify the administrator or maintenance coordinator of a new request.
  FR-29: The coordinator shall contact the landlord or authorized property manager for approval.
  FR-30: The system shall record the landlord’s approval or rejection.
  FR-31: A repairer shall not be assigned before landlord approval has been recorded.
  FR-32: Only approved and active repairer accounts shall be eligible for assignment.
  FR-33: After approval, the coordinator shall assign an eligible repairer.
  FR-34: Students shall be able to view repair request status.
  FR-35: Repair statuses shall include Submitted, Awaiting landlord approval, Approved, Rejected, Repairer
 assigned, In progress, Completed, and Cancelled.
 FR-36: The system shall record the assigned repairer and relevant status changes.
 
3.6 Cleaning Services
  FR-37: Students shall be able to request cleaning services.
  FR-38: Cleaning requests shall include location, preferred date, service type, and optional notes.
  FR-39: Administrators shall be able to manage cleaner accounts.
  FR-40: Only approved cleaners shall be assigned to cleaning requests.
  FR-41: Students shall be able to view cleaning request status.
 
3.7 Student Job Listings
  FR-42: Employers and administrators shall be able to publish student job listings.

 application instructions.
 FR-44: Students shall be able to search and filter jobs by location, job type, and keywords.
  FR-45: Students shall be able to apply through the platform or follow the employer’s application instructions.

3.8 Dashboards
  FR-47: Student dashboards shall display saved listings, inquiries, repair requests, cleaning requests, and job

 FR-48: Landlord dashboards shall support property management and viewing inquiries and repair approval
 requests.
 FR-49: Repairer dashboards shall display assigned jobs and status.

3.9 Notifications
  FR-53: The system shall notify users when relevant request statuses change.
  FR-54: Notifications may be delivered through in-platform notifications, email, or SMS, depending on available
 integrations.
 FR-55: The system shall record important notifications and delivery status where supported.
 
4. Non-Functional Requirements
4.1 Performance
  NFR-01: Main pages should load within 3 seconds under normal network conditions.
  NFR-02: Search results should be returned within 3 seconds under normal operating conditions.
  NFR-03: The system shall support multiple simultaneous users.
  NFR-04: Images shall be optimized to reduce data usage.
 
4.2 Security
  NFR-05: Passwords shall be securely hashed.
  NFR-06: The production platform shall use HTTPS.
  NFR-07: Users shall only access information and functions permitted by their roles.
  NFR-08: Administrative actions and repair approval decisions shall be recorded.
  NFR-09: User input shall be validated to reduce common security risks.
  NFR-10: Private user information shall not be publicly displayed without authorization.
 
4.3 Reliability and Availability
  NFR-11: The production platform should target at least 99% monthly availability, excluding scheduled
 maintenance.
 NFR-12: The system shall maintain regular database backups.
  NFR-13: The system shall provide clear error messages when an operation fails.

 NFR-14: The system shall prevent duplicate or unauthorized repair assignments.
 
4.4 Usability and Accessibility
  NFR-15: The interface shall be easy to use for students with basic digital skills.
  NFR-16: The platform shall be responsive on mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
  NFR-17: The system shall use readable fonts, clear buttons, and consistent navigation.
  NFR-18: Important actions shall provide confirmation or feedback.
 
4.5 Localization
  NFR-19: The default currency shall be FCFA.
  NFR-20: The platform shall support Cameroon regions, cities, neighborhoods, and institutions.
  NFR-21: The initial interface shall be available in English, with French support planned or included in localization.
 
5. Data Requirements
The system shall store and manage the following data:
  User accounts and roles
  Student profiles
  Landlord and property manager profiles
  Accommodation listings and photographs
  Institution and location records
  Geographic coordinates
  Accommodation inquiries and booking requests
  Repair requests and landlord approval records
  Repairer profiles and assignments
  Cleaner profiles and cleaning requests
  Job listings and applications
  Notifications and administrative activity logs
 The database shall maintain relationships between users, properties, requests, and service providers.
6. Business Rules
  BR-01: Accommodation prices shall be displayed in FCFA.
  BR-02: A repair request must be linked to a property or valid accommodation location.
  BR-03: Landlord approval is mandatory before a repairer can be assigned.
  BR-04: Only approved repairers may receive repair assignments.
  BR-05: Only approved cleaners may receive cleaning assignments.
  BR-06: Listings shall not be marked verified until verification is complete.
  BR-07: Administrators shall be able to remove fraudulent, misleading, or inappropriate listings.
  BR-08: The platform shall maintain a record of repair approval and assignment decisions.

7. External Interfaces
7.1 User Interface
  Home page
  Accommodation search and details pages
  Interactive map
  Student and landlord dashboards
  Repair request page
  Cleaning request page
  Student jobs page
  Service provider dashboard
  Administrator dashboard
 
7.2 Map Interface
The platform shall integrate a mapping service capable of displaying geographic locations across Cameroon.
7.3 Notification Interface
The platform may integrate email, SMS, or other notification services.
7.4 Payment Interface
If online booking or deposits are introduced, the platform may integrate supported payment providers used in
Cameroon. Payment processing is outside the initial scope unless separately approved.
8. Acceptance Criteria
  Students can register and log in.
  Students can search accommodation by institution and location.
  Searching “Hostels near RHIBMS” returns relevant listings near RHIBMS.
  Accommodation prices are displayed in FCFA.
  Accommodation listings appear on the map when valid coordinates exist.
  Landlords can create and manage property listings.
  Students can submit repair requests.
  Repair requests cannot be assigned until landlord approval is recorded.
  Only approved repairers can be assigned.
  Students can submit cleaning requests.
  Students can browse and apply for jobs.
  Administrators can manage users, listings, jobs, and service requests.
  The platform works on common mobile and desktop browsers.
  User data and role permissions are protected.

10. Conclusion
CampusNest shall provide a centralized accommodation and student services platform for Cameroon. It will help
students discover accommodation, connect with landlords, request approved maintenance services, access
cleaning services, and find student employment opportunities. The platform shall prioritize usability, security,
reliable information, location-based search, and a clear landlord approval process for repairs.
